- id: [PDP111](https://github.com/mabablue/I-ADOPT-patterns-playground/blob/main/pattern/PDP111.md)
- name: property (cover)
- definition: Cover expresses the fraction of a total reference area that is occupied by the Object of Interest.
- design: Asymmetric system (numerator/denominator) at the object of interest slot. The portion is represented by the numerator, the total reference area by the denominator.
